### Full description

```text
Synapse Dojo is a collection of free brain training games. Challenge your mind for a few minutes each day with 40 short exercises inspired by classic cognitive science research, with no ads and no sign-up.

Inspired by Classic Cognitive Science
• Train across 9 domains: speed, attention, executive control, working memory, episodic memory, spatial cognition, reasoning and planning, math and reading, and bilateral coordination.
• Play game versions of classic neuropsychological tasks such as the Stroop test, N-Back, Corsi blocks, Tower of London, mental rotation and the UFOV task from the ACTIVE study.
• Try exercises you won't find elsewhere, like Echo, a dual N-Back task with a spoken letter, and Bilateral Rhythm, where you tap with both hands to an audio-visual metronome.
• Discover where each exercise comes from: a "What it's based on" card names the research paradigm and the reference study.
• Stay challenged with adaptive difficulty that adjusts in real time, using a psychophysical staircase that keeps you at 70–85% accuracy, so exercises are never too easy or too frustrating.

Your Daily Mental Workout
• Daily Workout: 10–15 minutes, with 4 exercises chosen from the skills you trained least recently.
• Free Gym: play any of the 40 games whenever you like.
• Neuro Score: a daily 0–1000 summary across all domains to follow your own trend over the weeks. It is not a clinical test.
• Every exercise has a step-by-step "How to play" guide, an illustrated example and a practice round that does not count.
• Add a daily reminder to your phone's calendar from Settings, with no notifications from the app.

Rewards for Consistent Effort
• Earn XP, level up and collect more than 50 badges.
• Streaks without stress: missing a day never takes away what you earned, and 2–3 sessions a week are enough.
• Optional weekly leaderboard (off by default) that rewards consistency and improvement, under a random nickname.
• Private groups let friends and family encourage each other to keep going.

Designed for Everyone
• Relaxed pace mode gives 40% more time on timed exercises.
• Large text option and a light or dark theme that follows your phone.
• After the first launch the exercises also work without an internet connection.
• 25 languages, with word games adapted to each alphabet.

Free and Private
• Completely free: no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no notifications.
• No account, no e-mail, no cookies, no profiling.
• Your progress is saved only on your device. The app sends only anonymous counts with no identifier; the optional leaderboard and the transfer code send more only if you use them.
• Export a backup file at any time, or move to a new device with a temporary code.

Synapse Dojo is made for adults and older adults who enjoy puzzles and want a short daily mental routine, and for anyone curious about the classic experiments of cognitive psychology.

Synapse Dojo is designed for personal enjoyment and curiosity. It is not a medical device and does not claim to diagnose, treat, prevent or cure any condition.
```

## Dove incollarli

### Google Play

1. Play Console › l'app › **Crescita › Presenza sullo store › Schede dello store** › **Scheda principale dello store**.
2. In inglese (lingua predefinita) sostituisci **Nome dell'app**, **Descrizione breve** e **Descrizione completa** con
   i testi inglesi qui sopra.
3. In **Gestisci traduzioni** (o il selettore della lingua in alto) apri **Italiano – it-IT** e incolla i testi italiani.
4. **Salva**. Le modifiche alla scheda vanno in revisione da sole, senza una nuova release; con la *Pubblicazione
   gestita* disattivata escono appena approvate.

### Huawei AppGallery

1. AppGallery Connect › l'app › **Distribute › App information**.
2. In inglese: **App name** può restare `Synapse Dojo` o diventare `Synapse Dojo: Brain Training` (massimo 64
   caratteri); **Brief introduction** = descrizione breve; **Full introduction** = descrizione completa.
3. Passa all'italiano dal selettore della lingua (o *Manage languages*) e incolla i testi italiani.
4. **Save**, poi **Submit for review**. Se chiede una versione, va bene la 0.21.1 già pubblicata.

### Uptodown

Lasciare finire la revisione in corso (*Pending revision*): modificare la scheda adesso può farla ripartire. Dopo
l'approvazione, dall'icona di modifica dell'app aggiorna i campi della descrizione in inglese e italiano, se la console
li offre: su Uptodown i testi pubblicati li rivedono e spesso li riscrivono gli editor.

## Google Play: modulo per l'accesso alla produzione

Alla fine dei 14 giorni di test chiuso la Dashboard della Play Console propone il modulo per chiedere l'accesso alla
produzione. Risposte basate su ciò che è successo davvero (report di Testers Community del 25 novembre 2026 e
versioni 0.24.0–0.24.3 del sito), in inglese. Dove c'è una scelta tua, è indicata tra parentesi quadre.

**1. How did you recruit users for your closed test?**

```text
I used a paid testing provider (Testers Community), which supplied the testers for the 14-day closed test and a written report with their feedback. [If true, add: I also shared the app with a few friends and family members who match the target audience.]
```

**2. How easy was it to recruit testers for your app?** [Scegli tu; con un fornitore a pagamento di solito *Easy*.]

**3. Describe the engagement you received from testers during your closed test**

```text
Testers installed the app on a range of devices and Android versions and used its main features: the daily workout, the free gym with its 40 exercises, progress screens and settings. No crashes or blocking bugs were reported. They sent five concrete suggestions about navigation, the Progress screen, quitting exercises, the store description and sharing, which I fixed during the test.
```

**4. Provide a summary of the feedback that you received from testers. Include how you collected the feedback.**

```text
Feedback was collected through the provider's written test report. Testers found the app stable on all devices, with no crashes. They suggested: (1) the Android Back button closed the app instead of going to the previous screen; (2) quitting an exercise with the ✕ button had no confirmation; (3) the Progress screen showed a "Top skill" at 0% before any exercise; (4) there was no way to rate or share the app; (5) the store description was short and hard to scan.
```

**5. Who is the intended audience for your app?**

```text
Adults and older adults who enjoy puzzles and want a short daily mental routine of 10–15 minutes, and anyone curious about classic cognitive psychology tasks. The app is free, has no ads, needs no account and is available in 25 languages. It is a game for enjoyment and curiosity, not a medical or diagnostic tool.
```

**6. Describe how your app provides value to the users.**

```text
It offers 40 short games inspired by classic cognitive psychology tasks (Stroop, N-Back, Corsi blocks, Tower of London and others), with difficulty that adapts to each player, a daily workout, a personal score to follow one's own trend, and a card for each exercise explaining the research it comes from. Everything is free, with no ads, no account and progress kept on the device.
```

**7. How many installs do you expect your app to have in your first year?** [Scegli tu una stima realistica.]

**8. What changes did you make to your app based on what you learned during your closed test?**

```text
The Android Back button now goes to the previous screen, leaves an exercise, and on the home screen asks to press Back again before closing the app. Quitting an exercise with ✕ needs a second tap within a few seconds. The Progress screen no longer names a "Top skill" before any exercise. Settings has a new section to rate the app, share it and send a suggestion. The store description was rewritten and expanded. These changes were released in versions 0.24.0 to 0.24.3 and checked on a real device.
```

**9. How did you decide that your app is ready for production?**

```text
Testers reported no crashes or blocking bugs on any device; every issue they raised was fixed and verified on a real Android phone; and the app's content, privacy policy and data safety answers are complete and consistent.
```

```text
This is my first app on Google Play. I tested it with a closed group of testers on many devices before asking for production access, and acted on all of their feedback before applying.
```
